## What Is a eu.cc Domain?

`eu.cc` is essentially a second-level domain based on the `.cc` top-level domain, offered by the registrar Gname. It isn't an ICANN-approved top-level domain, but in practice it behaves just like one: it fully supports DNS records, and registration, renewal, and transfer all work the same way as on a regular TLD. For most use cases — testing, personal sites, or hobby projects — it's perfectly serviceable, and the fact that it's free to register makes it very appealing.

## Registration Steps

### Step 1: Visit the Gname Official Website

Head over to Gname's official site at `https://www.gname.com/`. Open the **Domain Registration (域名注册)** menu at the top of the page and click the **Free Registration (免费注册)** entry to get to the promotion page.

### Step 2: Register an Account

If you don't have a Gname account yet, sign up with your email address (Gmail or a Microsoft mailbox is recommended, and a Chinese mobile number works fine for phone verification). After your first registration, the site may prompt you to add at least one contact method — if you don't want to, you can simply close that prompt and move on.

### Step 3: Claim the Free Coupon

On the free registration page, click the **免费注册** (Free Registration) button. The system will automatically issue you a set of eu.cc full-discount registration coupons — enough to cover **3 free eu.cc registrations**. Keep in mind the coupons are only valid for **30 days** from the moment you claim them, so use them before they expire.

### Step 4: Search and Register Your Domain

Enter the domain prefix you want in the search box. When the results come back, look for one whose status shows **Not Registered (未注册)** and whose registration price shows **Promo (促销)**. If both conditions are met, your registration can go through at zero cost; otherwise the domain may not be registrable for free, or you'd have to pay for it. Click to register, confirm the checkout shows an amount due of **$0.00**, and complete the order.

### Step 5: Set Up DNS Resolution

Once the registration succeeds, head to **My Domains (我的域名)** and manage your new domain. You can configure DNS records here directly, or take the domain to another DNS provider. If you want to make the most of it, you can also point the domain's nameservers to Cloudflare (more on that below) and enjoy free CDN and HTTPS afterward.

## Important Notes

### Quota Limits

Not everyone gets the same number of free domains. Regular users can register **3 eu.cc domains** free, basic resellers get **10**, and advanced resellers get up to **20** (including the slots granted during the basic-reseller stage).

### Premium Domain Restrictions

According to the registry's requirements, so-called premium domains — such as two-letter names like `AA.eu.cc`, three-letter names like `ABC.eu.cc`, and brand words (well-known company names or popular industry terms) — **cannot** be registered with the eu.cc full-discount coupon. The actual price for those will be shown when you search, so check before you commit.

### Free Renewal

The good news is that renewals are free too — but with a few conditions. You can only submit a free renewal within **90 days before the domain expires**, and it must be done through the **Free Renewal (免费续费)** section of the activity page; renewing from your normal domain list or via auto-renewal won't get you the free price. Premium domains are also excluded from free renewal.
