Raycast is a productivity powerhouse on the Mac — a launcher, a toolbox, and a scripting platform all rolled into one.

This guide walks you through getting the most out of Raycast.

## Why Choose Raycast

- Blazing-fast launch speed
- A rich ecosystem of extensions
- Built-in window management, clipboard history, and translation
- Support for custom scripts
- The free tier is already impressively powerful

## Installation

Download it from https://www.raycast.com, then set the shortcut `⌥ Space` to replace the default `⌘ Space`.
