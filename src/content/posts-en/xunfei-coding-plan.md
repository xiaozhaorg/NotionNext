Developers end up "using AI frugally" instead of "fully unleashing AI."

Today, the iFlytek Xingchen Coding Plan is getting a major upgrade.

## ¥3.9 Unlimited: From Token Anxiety to Worry-Free Usage

When "raising shrimp" or doing AI-assisted coding, the thing developers dread most is rate limiting and surprise bills from sudden high-concurrency workloads.

With this upgrade, billing has shifted from **per-token** to **per-request**.

On the surface it's just a change in billing unit, but for developers it's a genuine paradigm shift — you stop obsessing over "how many resources did I burn" and start thinking about "how many requests did I complete."

At the same time, targeting students, independent developers, and newcomers to AI coding, the original "Starter" tier has been upgraded into **"Worry-Free,"** which costs just ¥3.9 and comes with **unlimited requests**.

No matter how much work you pile onto your "lobster" in a single day, or how many requests you fire off, you'll never be brutally rate-limited for blowing past a request cap again. That's true "shrimp-farming freedom."

If the number of requests determines how many times you can "command" your shrimp, then the model determines just how capable the shrimp you raise really is.

**Qwen3-Coder-Next:**

With these new models, developers get far more "weapons" to choose from inside a single plan.

Reach for the strongest model on complex algorithms, switch to the most efficient one for daily completion — true multi-model free dispatch.
