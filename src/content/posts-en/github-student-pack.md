The GitHub Student Developer Pack is one of the best perks available to students. Dozens of paid services become completely free with it — the combined value easily tops $1,000. Here's a complete guide to getting the pack, covering who qualifies, how to apply, and which benefits are actually worth grabbing.

## 1. Application Requirements

- You must be a currently enrolled student.
- You need a school email address (a `.edu` address or one tied to your student ID).
- You'll need to upload your student ID or proof of enrollment.

## 2. Application Process

- Visit https://education.github.com/pack
- Click **"Get the Pack"**
- Log in to GitHub
- Verify your student status
- Upload your proof documents
- Wait for the review (usually 1-3 days)

## 3. The Best Freebies Worth Grabbing

### Cloud Services

- **DigitalOcean** — $200 in credit (about 14 months of a VPS)
- **Microsoft Azure** — $100 in credit
- **AWS Educate** — $100 plus extra resources
- **Heroku** — $13/month for 2 years

### Domains & Websites

- **Namecheap** — free 1-year `.me` domain
- **`.tech` domain** — free for 1 year
- **GitHub Pages** — free static hosting
- **Netlify** — free hosting

### Developer Tools

- **JetBrains** full suite — free (IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, and more)
- **GitHub Pro** — unlimited private repositories
- **GitHub Copilot** — free AI pair programming
- **DataCamp** — data science courses
- **Educative** — programming courses

### Design & Resources

- **Figma** — free on the professional plan
- **Canva** — free on the premium plan
- **Icons8** — icon assets

## 4. Things to Keep in Mind

- You'll lose some benefits after you graduate.
- Each service can only be claimed once.
- Some perks require a credit card for verification.
- Use your school email when signing up.

## 5. Recommended Combinations

- **JetBrains + GitHub Copilot** (programming)
- **DigitalOcean + Namecheap** (building a website)
- **Figma + Canva** (design)
- Getting the full bundle can save you a significant amount of money.
