## Under 20MB with Support for 40+ Databases!

This open-source DBX client is a must-have.

### Introduction: Is Navicat Too Expensive and DBeaver Too Bloated?

Have you been looking for a database client that is free and open source, lightweight and compact, yet still supports a wide range of mainstream databases?

Today, let me introduce a hidden gem I've come across — DBX.

### What Is DBX?

DBX is a cross-platform database client built on the Rust + Tauri 2 tech stack. It has a remarkably small footprint while supporting a rich variety of database types, including MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite, Redis, and more. Best of all, it's completely open source and free.

### Key Features of DBX

- **Ultra lightweight:** The installer comes in at under 20MB and takes up very little disk space.
- **Cross-platform:** Works on Windows, Linux, and macOS.
- **Low memory usage:** Thanks to the design of the Rust language and the Tauri 2 framework.
- **AI-powered:** Built-in AI features that double your database management efficiency.
- **Broad database support:** 40+ databases, including MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite, Redis, and more.
- **Cloud sync:** Supports WebDAV for syncing your configuration, so you don't have to set everything up again when you switch computers.
- **Code snippets:** Comes with common SQL snippets built in to boost development efficiency.
- **And more:** Continuously updated...

### How to Install

Visit the official website at [https://dbxio.com/cn](https://dbxio.com/cn), pick the installer that matches your operating system, and download and install it. That's all there is to it.

### Summary

If you're tired of Navicat's hefty price tag and DBeaver's bloated experience, give DBX a try — this open-source powerhouse weighs in at under 20MB.

It's not only free, lightweight, and cross-platform, but it also supports mainstream databases like MySQL and Redis, and even comes with an AI assistant and cloud-based configuration sync.
