If you follow ByteDance's TRAE lineup, you're probably already familiar with it — TraeCode is the AI-native IDE, while its "other half," **TraeWork**, is an AI platform focused on office work. Simply put: **you hand off a task, and it gets it done in the cloud** — from organizing documents and processing data to building web pages and putting together PPT decks, it's all within its wheelhouse.

This article covers what TraeWork is, how to use it, and how free users can stack up credits without spending a dime, with a new-user signup bonus at the end.

## 1. What Is TraeWork?

TraeWork is the **AI office platform** in ByteDance's TRAE ecosystem, complementing TraeCode (the AI coding IDE):

| Product | Focus | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| TraeCode | AI-native IDE | Writing code, fixing bugs, project development |
| TraeWork | AI office platform | Documents, spreadsheets, PPT, data processing, web page creation, and more |

Unlike running tasks on your local machine, TraeWork is built around **cloud execution**: once you create a task, it automatically processes it using cloud models. Your computer only needs a lightweight desktop client, and tasks can run in parallel in the background without eating up your local compute.

## 2. What Kinds of Tasks Can It Handle?

According to its official positioning, TraeWork targets "all kinds of tasks." Common use cases include:

- 📄 **Document processing**: summarizing long texts, rewriting and polishing, formatting cleanup
- 📊 **Data processing**: spreadsheet cleaning, data analysis, chart generation
- 🎨 **Content creation**: writing copy, building PPT decks, generating supporting images
- 🌐 **Web page creation**: generating a page straight from your requirements
- 🔧 **Automation for efficiency**: batch tasks, organizing materials, extracting information

You can think of it as a **cloud-based AI task workstation**: describe what you need clearly, and leave the rest to it.

## 4. My Hands-On Impressions

The most noticeable thing after using it is that it's **"task-based" rather than "conversation-based"**: instead of burning time in endless back-and-forth chat, you write out your requirements, submit the task, and let the cloud run it — go do your own thing and come back to check the results when it's done.
