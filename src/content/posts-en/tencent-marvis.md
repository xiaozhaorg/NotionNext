Tencent's Yingyongbao (App Store) team officially launched Marvis in May 2026 — an OS-level AI assistant that pays homage to Iron Man's Jarvis.

Unlike traditional chatbots, once Marvis is installed you'll see six little "workhorses" each doing their own job in a virtual office. These are Marvis's six built-in expert agents, standing by 24/7.

## Six Agents: An AI Team That Works for You

Marvis's most innovative feature is its multi-agent collaboration architecture.

These six agents have clearly defined roles:

- **Main Agent (PM):** Listens to your needs, breaks down tasks, and assigns them to the right specialists.
- **File Agent:** Handles file search, reading, format conversion, and document generation.
- **Computer Agent:** Manages low-level Windows configuration, hardware detection, and startup items.
- **App Agent:** Can invoke any application or EXE program on your computer.
- **Browser Agent:** Takes over web pages for data scraping and interaction.
- **Search Agent:** Finds information from public sources and includes citations.

When you issue a command, these agents work in parallel.

For example, say "help me figure out which startup programs are useless and turn them off." The Main Agent will coordinate multiple sub-agents at the same time to analyze the different programs, present its suggestions, and ask for your confirmation before actually executing anything.

## OS-Level Integration: Making AI Truly Your Computer

Marvis's core breakthrough is that it can reach directly into the Windows OS layer.

Traditional AI assistants are limited to surface-level operations, but Marvis connects directly to the system's low-level APIs, truly making "natural language equals system commands" a reality.

It turns your entire computer into a conversational smart object:

- Learn your computer's specs with a single sentence
- Change system settings
- Optimize startup items
- Clean up redundant files

It can even check whether your hardware can run a certain game smoothly, how healthy your battery is, and whether your network is working properly.

## Efficiency Mode and Privacy Mode: Switch as Needed

Marvis offers two working modes.

**Efficiency Mode** uses device-cloud collaboration: complex problem understanding and planning lean on cloud models, while file processing and execution actions are handled on-device whenever possible, making responses faster and more stable.

**Privacy Mode** uses an on-device model — all data parsing, image recognition, and conversation happen locally, with nothing uploaded to the cloud.

Interestingly, the device-cloud collaboration isn't a simple either/or choice. Instead, tasks are layered:

- Recognition, indexing, and preprocessing that can be done locally are handled locally
- Cloud capabilities are only invoked when necessary

This design both reduces token consumption and minimizes unnecessary data transfer.

## Remote Control from Your Phone: A Personal Computer in Your Pocket

Marvis supports Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android, with seamless sync across devices under the same account.

You can view your computer's screen directly on your phone and take over operations in real time.

Even when the computer is locked, you can remotely enter the password from your phone to unlock and control it.

Anything you can do on your computer, you can do remotely from your phone — truly creating a personal computer you can carry anywhere.

## 10 Million Tokens Per Day: Genuinely Free

Marvis launched with a major perk right out of the gate:

Every user gets 10 million free tokens per day — no paywall, no invite code, completely open and free.

For a typical user, daily office work, chatting, and system operations consume less than 100,000 tokens. That means 10 million tokens covers roughly 100 days of everyday use.

## Summary

The arrival of Marvis marks the evolution of AI assistants from chatbots to OS-level assistants.

It's not just an important experiment by Tencent in the AI space — it represents a new paradigm:

When AI can truly understand and control your computer, the traditional graphical interface interaction may be completely transformed.
