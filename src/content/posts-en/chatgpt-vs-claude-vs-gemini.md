## Introduction

In 2026, competition in the large language model market has reached a fever pitch.

OpenAI's GPT-5.6, Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 4, and Google's Gemini 2.5 have each evolved to new heights.

As a regular user faced with these three mainstream AIs, how do you choose?

This article compares them across five dimensions: coding ability, reasoning, creative writing, multimodal capabilities, and value for money.

## Coding Ability Comparison

When it comes to programming tasks, Claude Sonnet 4 is currently the best performer.

It doesn't just generate correct code—it also understands complex business logic and architecture design.

GPT-5.6 follows closely, delivering consistent performance on everyday coding tasks.

Gemini 2.5 has an edge when it comes to generating long code (it supports a 2 million token context window), but its code quality is slightly behind the others.

## Reasoning Ability Comparison

On mathematical reasoning and logic puzzles, GPT-5.6 and Claude 4 each have their strengths.

GPT-5.6 scores higher on math competition problems such as AIME, while Claude 4 is more stable on everyday tasks that demand multi-step reasoning, like financial analysis and project management.

Gemini 2.5 excels at scientific reasoning in fields like physics and chemistry, thanks to Google's accumulated academic data.

## Creative Writing Comparison

Claude 4 is clearly ahead in creative writing.

Its prose feels more natural and emotionally richer, and the output reads more like something written by a human.

GPT-5.6's writing is more structured, making it a good fit for business copy and technical documentation.

Gemini 2.5 has an advantage in multilingual writing, supporting high-quality translation and creation in 100+ languages.

## Multimodal Capabilities

All three models support image input, but each has a different focus:

- **GPT-5.6:** The most comprehensive image understanding, covering OCR, object recognition, and scene description
- **Claude 4:** More precise at interpreting charts and documents, ideal for business analysis
- **Gemini 2.5:** The strongest video understanding, capable of analyzing long video content

### Value for Money

Looking at pricing (per million tokens):

- **GPT-5.6:** $2.5 input / $10 output (OpenAI official API)
- **Claude 4:** $3 input / $15 output (Anthropic official API)
- **Gemini 2.5:** $1.25 input / $5 output (Google official API)

Gemini 2.5 offers the best value for money, although access may be limited from within mainland China.

GPT-5.6 and Claude 4 are similarly priced, so the choice comes down to your use case.

## How to Choose?

### Choose ChatGPT (GPT-5.6) if you:
- Need the most comprehensive AI capabilities
- Value the plugin ecosystem and third-party integrations
- Want DALL-E image generation

### Choose Claude if you:
- Have coding and code review as your primary needs
- Need to process long documents (Claude supports a 200k token context window)
- Care about AI safety and controllability

### Choose Gemini if you:
- Have a limited budget and prioritize value for money
- Need multilingual support and translation
- Need video analysis capabilities

### New Trends in 2026

All three models are moving toward a "multimodal + reasoning + tool use" direction.

GPT-5.6 introduced Operator, which can automatically perform browser actions; Claude 4 strengthened its code generation and review capabilities; Gemini 2.5 leads in video understanding and generation.

Competition will only intensify in the future, and users will be the biggest beneficiaries.

## Summary

In 2026, there is no absolute "best AI"—only the AI that best fits your needs.

Pick Claude for coding, GPT-5.6 for an all-around performer, and Gemini for the best value.

A good approach is to choose your primary AI based on your main use case, and subscribe to 2–3 others as complements to make the most of each model's strengths.

For developers, the Claude + GPT-5.6 combination covers nearly all needs.
